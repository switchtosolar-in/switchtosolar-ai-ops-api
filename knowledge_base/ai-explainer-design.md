# AI Explainer Design

The AI Explainer provides explanations for individual sections of a solar report.

For example:

- Monthly savings
- Payback period
- System size
- Energy generation

Instead of free-form chat, it focuses on explaining a specific metric or section.

It uses structured prompts and predefined context to ensure consistent and accurate explanations.

The goal is to make solar concepts easy to understand for non-technical users.
